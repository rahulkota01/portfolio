About Me

Academic Profile

I am a third-year Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D) student at QIS College of Pharmacy, Andhra Pradesh, India, affiliated with JNTUK. My developing research interests lie in cancer biology, molecular oncology, cancer genetics and the cellular mechanisms underlying cancer progression.

I am particularly interested in understanding how genetic alterations and disrupted molecular pathways influence cancer-cell behaviour, including invasion, metastasis and treatment response.

My early research experiences have exposed me to cancer-related literature, miRNA–mRNA regulatory networks, molecular data analysis and nanocarrier-based therapeutic concepts. These experiences have encouraged me to explore cancer as a biological problem that requires investigation at genetic, molecular and cellular levels.

I am interested in gaining deeper experimental research experience and developing the scientific skills required to investigate cancer mechanisms. I also value interdisciplinary collaboration and see computational and analytical methods as useful tools for supporting biological research.
