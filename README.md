# Academic Researcher Portfolio - Rahul Kota (Pharm.D)

A modern, high-impact, editorial-grade academic researcher portfolio website crafted for **Rahul Kota** (Doctor of Pharmacy candidate & Bio-Computational Researcher). Built using clean semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 (Variables, Glassmorphism, CSS Grid/Flexbox), Lucide Icons, and interactive JavaScript.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Design Features

1. **Modern Academic & Research Aesthetics**:
   - Dark/Light Theme switcher with persistent user preference (`localStorage`).
   - Dynamic bioluminescent particle background grid tailored for biomedical & computational research.
   - Clean typography using Google Fonts (**Plus Jakarta Sans** & **Space Grotesk**).

2. **Structured Academic Sections**:
   - **Hero Banner**: Professional headshot avatar, research badge, location chip, and direct contact CTAs.
   - **Academic Profile & Aspirations**: Highlights Pharm.D background, GATE (XL & BT) focus, and target research destinations (IISc, IITs, top international labs).
   - **6 Core Research Domain Cards**:
     - Computational Genomics & Bioinformatics
     - AI/ML for Biological & Clinical Data
     - CRISPR & Functional Genomics
     - Cancer Biology & Systems Biology
     - Targeted Nanocarrier Delivery
     - Computational Biological Systems
   - **Featured Projects & Publications**:
     - *miRNA Nanocarrier Delivery in TNBC Treatment* (Guided Mentorship under PhD scholar at **IIT BHU**).
     - *AI-Assisted Healthcare Utility Website* (Live deployed Streamlit app for clinical dosage & BMI).
     - *Marine-Derived Extracellular Vesicles as Nanocarriers* (Review Article - First Author under Dr. B. Revathi).
     - *Nootropic Chocolate Candy Delivery System* (Formulation Science proposal).
   - **Timeline (Education & Achievements)**:
     - 1st Place Poster Presentation Award at National Pharmacy Week 2025.
     - 10-Day GIAN Course at **IIT Indore** (Collab with Purdue University, USA).
     - 5th HEALTH CARE SUMMIT 2025 at **IISc Bangalore**.
     - NPTEL Mathematical Foundations for Machine Learning (**IISc - NITK**).
   - **Technical Skills & Languages Bar**:
     - Python, Machine Learning, SQL, Java progress meters.
     - Language cards: English, Hindi, German (A1 CEFR Level).
